## Key dates for 2027

| What | When |
| --- | --- |
| HSC (Class 12) practicals, orals, internal assessment | 20 Jan to 6 Feb 2027 |
| HSC (Class 12) written papers | 8 Feb to 16 Mar 2027 |
| SSC (Class 10) practicals, orals, internal assessment | 28 Jan to 13 Feb 2027 |
| SSC (Class 10) written papers | 15 Feb to 11 Mar 2027 |
| Subject-wise timetable | Not yet released. It will be added here as soon as the board publishes it. |

<p class="small">As announced by the Maharashtra State Board on 17 Sep 2026. Always confirm on <a href="https://mahahsscboard.in">mahahsscboard.in</a> and with your school.</p>

## Free official resources

- **Board website and notices:** [mahahsscboard.in](https://mahahsscboard.in)
- **Sample question papers from the board:** on mahahsscboard.in, open Student Login, then Sample Question Paper, then choose HSC or SSC and the subject.
- **All Maharashtra Board textbooks as free PDFs (Balbharati):** [books.ebalbharati.in](https://books.ebalbharati.in/)

## Free textbook solutions

Shaalaa.com publishes free chapter-wise answers to the Maharashtra Board (SCERT) textbooks. Use them to check answers after trying a question yourself, not instead of trying.

- **Class 10:** [Algebra (Maths 1)](https://www.shaalaa.com/textbook-solutions/scert-maharashtra-solutions-algebra-mathematics-1-english-10-standard-ssc_414), [Science and Technology Part 1](https://www.shaalaa.com/textbook-solutions/scert-maharashtra-solutions-science-and-technology-part-1-english-standard-10-maharashtra-state-board_416), [History and Political Science](https://shaalaa.com/textbook-solutions/scert-maharashtra-solutions-history-and-political-science-social-science-english-10-standard-ssc_407)
- **Class 12, all subjects:** [SCERT Maharashtra solutions for 12th Standard](https://www.shaalaa.com/textbook-solutions/a/scert-maharashtra-solutions_408)
- **All classes and subjects, with past papers:** [Maharashtra Board study material](https://shaalaa.com/study-material/maharashtra-board_3186)
