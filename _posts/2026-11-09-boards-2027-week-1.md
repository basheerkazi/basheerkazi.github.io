One short note every Monday for families with a child in Class 10 or 12 this year. Here is what matters this week.

## Where we are

HSC written papers start on 8 February, SSC on 15 February. That is about 13 weeks for HSC and 14 for SSC. Right now the only job is to **finish the syllabus by the end of November**, including the chapters your child has been avoiding.

## This week's 10-minute task

Sit with your child and open the contents page of one textbook. Next to each chapter, give a mark out of five: five means they could teach it, one means they avoid it. Anything three or below goes on a weak-chapter list. One weak chapter a day, first thing in each study session, clears about thirty chapters by the end of December.

## Free resource of the week

The board's own sample question papers. On [mahahsscboard.in](https://mahahsscboard.in), open Student Login, then Sample Question Paper, then choose HSC or SSC. Download them now; they become the main practice tool from late December. Every free link we recommend is kept on the [Boards 2027 Help Desk](/boards-2027/) page.
