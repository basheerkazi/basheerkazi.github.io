One short note every Monday for families with a child in Class 10 or 12 this year. Here is what matters this week.

## Where we are

HSC written papers start on 8 February, SSC on 15 February. That is 12 weeks for HSC and 13 for SSC. We are still in the **finish the syllabus** phase, and it ends on 29 November. Two weeks to go. Keep clearing one weak chapter a day from last week's list.

## This week's 10-minute task

Check your child's board exam form. News reports say the board has extended the form deadline to **30 November** for SSC and HSC students. Ask the class teacher or college office to show you what was submitted, and check four things: the spelling of your child's name, the mother's name, the photo, and the list of subjects. A spelling mistake on the form ends up on the hall ticket and the marksheet. It is far easier to fix now than in February.

## Free resource of the week

Lost a textbook, or the school copy is falling apart? Every Maharashtra Board textbook is on the Balbharati e-book library as a PDF: [books.ebalbharati.in](https://books.ebalbharati.in/). Choose the standard, medium and subject, then download. Handy for finishing those last chapters on a phone or a printout. All our free links are on the [Boards 2027 Help Desk](/boards-2027/).

## Coming up

Several education news sites report that the board released the subject-wise timetable on 7 November. The first papers match the board's earlier dates: HSC English on 8 February and SSC first language on 15 February. But some of the later dates in those reports do not match what the board announced in September, so we are not printing them here yet. Ask your school for the official timetable PDF, and we will add the confirmed dates to the Help Desk as soon as we can check them on the board's website.
