2. What AHILLA is, and what it is not

AHILLA is a calendar. It shows Hijri and Gregorian dates, prayer and fasting times, moon forecasts and moon-sighting announcements, and helps you keep track of personal and family dates.

  • Prayer, sehri and iftar times are calculated from astronomical formulas and the settings you choose. They are a guide. Please follow your local masjid when in doubt.
  • Hijri dates follow the moon committee you choose, or a calculated calendar. Local moon sighting can differ by a day.
  • Moon Watch forecasts are astronomy, not a religious ruling. The start of a month is decided by the committee you follow.
  • Religious content (duas, azkaar, iddah and other dates) is provided for convenience. It is not a fatwa. For rulings, please ask a scholar you trust.
