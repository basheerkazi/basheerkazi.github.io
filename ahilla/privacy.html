AHILLA Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Applies to the AHILLA app for Android

In short: AHILLA works on your phone. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells your data. You don't need an account. The only things that leave your phone are the ones you choose to share, and, if you use Family Wall, the family dates you add there.

Who we are

AHILLA is a Muslim calendar app made by Basheer Kazi in Pune, India. For any privacy question or request, write to ahilla.app@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device. We never receive them:

  • Your settings: language, city, prayer-time method, Hijri date adjustment, moon committee and reminder choices.
  • Your location, if you tap "Use my location". The app asks for approximate location only, uses it to work out prayer times and moon positions on the phone, and does not send it anywhere.
  • Your Ramadan fasting chart, Qur'an progress and Qaza ledger.
  • Life events you enter (names and dates of births, passings and family occasions).

Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

What the app downloads

AHILLA downloads a small public file of moon-sighting announcements from basheerkazi.github.io, which is hosted by GitHub. The app sends nothing about you in that request. Like any website, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Family Wall (optional)

Family Wall lets a family share a calendar. If you use it, the app stores the following on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, data located in Mumbai, India):

WhatWho can see it
Family name and its 6-letter invite codeMembers of that family
The display name you type for yourselfMembers of that family
Dates you add: title, date, how it repeats, and who added itMembers of that family
A random anonymous ID for your phoneUsed only to check that you are a member

You don't give an email address, phone number or account. Anyone you give your family's invite code to can join, so share it only with your family. Firebase is run by Google under the Firebase privacy terms.

Sharing

When you tap a share button (a day card, a moon-sighting report or a Family Wall invite), the app opens your phone's share menu, and you choose where it goes, for example WhatsApp. We don't see what you share.

Google Calendar

If you tap "Add to Google Calendar", Google Calendar opens and subscribes you to our public Hijri-date calendar. That subscription is between you and Google.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, for sehri, iftar and life-event reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders (optional), so sehri reminders arrive on time.
  • Approximate location (optional), for prayer times where you are.
  • Internet, for moon announcements and Family Wall.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Family Wall: open More, then Family Wall, and tap "Leave this family wall" to remove yourself. Any member can delete dates. To delete a whole family wall and everything in it, email ahilla.app@gmail.com with the family's invite code, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

AHILLA is a family calendar used by parents and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Parents may add family dates, such as a child's birth, on their own phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
