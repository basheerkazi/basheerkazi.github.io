I'm Basheer Kazi, based in Pune. For over 20 years I have built and run learning organisations: schools, coaching institutes and training programmes, including competitive exam coaching for NEET, JEE and CET.

I see myself as an organisation builder more than a trainer. My work today is guiding three groups who often get the least clear advice: **parents**, **students** and **job seekers**.

This site is where I share what I know, plainly and for free: board exam planning, choosing a stream or a school, and the skills that help young people get and keep a job.
